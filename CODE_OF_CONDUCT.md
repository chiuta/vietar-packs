# Code of Conduct

**English** · [Română](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ro.md) · [Français](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.fr.md) · [Italiano](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.it.md) · [Español](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md) · [Português](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.pt.md) · [Deutsch](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.de.md) · [Magyar](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.hu.md)

We follow the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1: be respectful, welcome newcomers, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated. Maintainers may remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules. Report problems to alexio@trom.tf.
